Dear Tomisin,

And you were one of the best parts of mine.

PROMISE ACCEPTED

Thank you for choosing us.

I can’t promise life won’t get busy or that we’ll talk every single day. But I can promise I won’t let one quiet week turn us into strangers.

I’ll still send the random message. I’ll still check in. I’ll still want to hear every unnecessarily detailed story.

I’m going to miss you more than these words can hold.
